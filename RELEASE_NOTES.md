**Concorde is now ConcordeAI.** New name, new lockup with the **AI** in bold, new app icon — same app, same private-by-default machine underneath.

- **It checks for updates by itself now.** Leave the app open for a week and it will still notice a new build, instead of waiting for you to click Check for updates.
- **The waiting is honest.** The progress bar glides instead of jumping, tells you roughly how long is left, and stays out of the way entirely on quick answers. When a first draft is ready you can hit **Answer now** and take the fast answer instead of the polished one.
- **Settings got a lot fuller.** A new Account pane (who you're signed in as, and the way out), a real model roster showing what's loaded and what each model is for, contribution controls that actually do what they promise, and release notes right in the app — like the ones you're reading.
- **Forget Me finally means it.** It used to clear only remembered facts while implying more. Now you choose exactly what goes, confirm it's you, and type the words.
- **Funnels take your own answer.** Typing something the option cards didn't offer now continues the funnel instead of dropping you into a wall of generic advice.
- **A seamless dark window** — the pale macOS title bar is gone.

Under the hood: a security fix worth mentioning — any web page you visited could previously have told this app, running on your own machine, to delete things. It can't any more.
